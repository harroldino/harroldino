Minimal Zen

A huge clock, the date, the weather and six apps on a slowly moving dark background. Nothing else. Everything you don't use daily sits behind the dots tile.

  • Customisable: which six apps show, their order, wallpaper, clock style.
  • Amazon's apps are hidden in "All apps", not deleted.

Command Centre

Everything at a glance: weather, what's live on your IPTV with a preview, scores for the teams you follow, your apps, and how the stick itself is doing.

  • Customisable: each widget can be moved, resized or switched off. Teams and channels are your own picks.
  • IPTV widget is where your own IPTV app (part 2) plugs in.

Combined

Zen is the default screen, with a small glance line under the clock. Press down past the apps and the dashboard slides up; press up or Back to tuck it away.

  • Try the customise button (top right): accent colour, 12/24-hour clock, glance line on or off, wallpaper.
  • Best of both: calm when you just want Netflix, all the info one press away.
