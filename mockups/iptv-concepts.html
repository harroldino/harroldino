My TV: 10 ideas for the IPTV app

Ten directions for the Fire Stick IPTV app that the My TV tile on Command Centre opens. Each screen is drawn at TV shape. The pictures are placeholders for live video and channel art. Pick the ones you like and we can mix them.

01Classic guide

A cable-box TV guide done properly. Live preview top left, what's on now in the middle, the full grid underneath.

LIVECh 102 · News 247:42 pm
Evening Bulletin
7:00 to 8:00 pm · National and world news, sport and weather
Next: 8:00 Hard Questions · 9:00 Late Edition
  • 7-day guide from your provider's XMLTV feed
  • Live preview keeps playing while you browse
  • Press OK on a future show to set a reminder
  • Type a channel number on the remote to jump
  • Groups from your playlist: News, Sport, Movies, Kids
  • Hide channels you never watch
FamiliarBest for big playlists

06Command Centre terminal

Matches the green terminal look of your home screen. Text-first, very fast, and you can jump anywhere by typing.

> mytv --group sport · 48 channels · 19:42
now : A-League · SYD 2-1 MVC
next : 21:00 Match Review
sig : 1080p · 6.2 Mbps · 0 drops
[OK] watch [☰] fav [◀] groups
> _
  • Same fonts and colours as Command Centre
  • Start typing on the remote's on-screen keys to filter
  • Shows stream health: resolution, bitrate, dropped frames
  • Themes: Matrix green, Teletext, Brutal
  • Tiny and fast, good for the older Fire TV Stick Lite
On brandLightweight

07Kids corner

A separate profile with only the channels you allow, big friendly tiles and a timer. A PIN gets you back to the grown-up view.

🦊Hi Mia! ⏱ 35 min of TV left
🔒 Grown-ups: hold Menu for 3 seconds
  • Allow-list of kids' channels only
  • Daily time limit with a friendly "time's up" screen
  • 4-digit PIN to leave the profile
  • Bedtime: TV stops at a set time
  • Big tiles with pictures, no small text
ProfilesCan be added to any design
