Matrix

The Command Centre layout you liked, rebuilt as a green-on-black terminal. Code rain falls behind the panels, everything is monospace, and the thing you're on lights up solid green.

  • Details: CRT scanlines, corner brackets on each panel, a blinking cursor, storage shown as a text bar.
  • Customisable: rain speed and density, glow strength, or swap green for amber or blue.

Teletext

Rogue one: your home screen as an old 1980s teletext page. Eight colours, chunky pixel type, page numbers for every app, and a blocky pixel picture for the live channel.

  • Details: page header with live clock, coloured section bars, and the four coloured shortcut buttons along the bottom.
  • Could go further: type a page number on the remote (101 for Netflix) to jump straight there.

Brutal

Rogue two: a loud, bright poster. Thick black borders, hard shadows, a giant temperature, and app tiles stuck on like stickers. Nothing like a normal TV menu.

  • Details: each widget gets its own colour block, the apps straighten up and pop when you land on them.
  • Customisable: swap the colour set, or turn the sticker tilt off.
